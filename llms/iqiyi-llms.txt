# iQIYI

> iQIYI is one of China's largest online video streaming and entertainment platforms, offering movies, TV dramas, variety shows, animation, and original content across web, mobile, and OTT devices. iQIYI operates a SaaS / OTT developer platform that lets partners launch their own branded streaming service, plus a QIYU XR developer platform for VR/AR device applications. This llms.txt was generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the iQIYI apis.yml catalog entry and public developer surface; iQIYI does not publish its own llms.txt.

## Developer

- [Developer Portal (iQIYI SaaS)](https://saas.iqiyi.com/): Launch a branded OTT/streaming platform on iQIYI SaaS.
- [Developer Documentation](https://saas.iqiyi.com/kszt/Developer_Documentation.html): Overview, Get Started, Authentication, RESTful API, Video, Channel, Album, Search, and Player API.
- [API Reference Index](https://saas.iqiyi.com/kszt/api_reference.html): RESTful API reference for Video, Channel, Category, and Album resources.
- [Getting Started / Tutorial](https://saas.iqiyi.com/kszt/getStarted.html): Onboarding tutorial for the SaaS RESTful API.
- [QIYU Developer Platform](https://dev-qiyu.iqiyi.com/): SDKs and tools for QIYU XR (VR/AR) device development (Unity, Unreal, Unity XR).
- [GitHub Organization](https://github.com/iqiyi): iQIYI open-source projects (xHook, xCrash, Qigsaw, dpvs, FASPell, and other Android/infrastructure/ML tooling).
